## What would change the letter

The score is four because NVIDIA closed more than fifteen percent below the May high while capital-spending guides stay up. It moves higher still if a named guarantee is restated, if a neocloud misses a payment, or if another large cheap-tier cut arrives without a volume offset.

The score moves to five if a residual-value guarantee is drawn, if a hyperscaler cuts capital spending, or if a hub credit event prints.

The score moves back to three only if the hub mark recovers inside the fifteen-percent line, mid-tier token prices stop falling, neocloud rental holds, and the circular book stops growing. A marketplace cell that stayed inside the cheap band is not that recovery. A Haiku name at ten cents is the other direction.

Wednesday’s cash and the tenth’s rental stamp confirmed a hub close about twenty-six and a half percent under the May high, a semiconductor index about twenty-eight percent under its June high, a rim equity mark still well under its high with unsecured notes at distressed yields, official flagship cards that held their lists, a cheap Haiku name still on the Anthropic card, a new Gemini flagship at an introductory list, and a rental series that left the neocloud H100 cell at three dollars thirty-seven and the marketplace cell at two dollars thirty-four. The conversion rate did not recover. Pipeline talk about stronger guarantees is not a draw. Commentary that no further backstop is required is not a filing.

## The open question

The loop is a race between a falling conversion rate and a rising stock of promises. The question worth keeping open is whether tokens sold, at these prices, can service paper that was sized on a higher price.

This letter does not tell the cash account what to own.
