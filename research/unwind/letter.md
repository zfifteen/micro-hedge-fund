## Circular paper and spender cash

NVIDIA's August filing still caps residual-value guarantees at the SB Energy Ohio site at one hundred five billion dollars, with OpenAI as the tenant. Amazon has closed fifty billion of OpenAI equity and added one hundred billion of AWS commercial commitment over eight years. That paper did not shrink over the weekend. Announced is not funded is not drawn. Drawn is the print that would move this letter from three to five.

Thursday's Reuters report still sits in a different drawer. Bankers and asset managers, speaking without names, said they want more certainty on a financing plan NVIDIA has described around chip-backed loans, and sources said deals still in the pipeline are likely to carry stronger guarantees. NVIDIA's own statement said its compute can support long-term financing and that partners assess each opportunity, including residual value. The company has also said some of those deals could carry no more than a twenty-five percent residual-value guarantee. Intent in a pipeline is not the August cap, and a lender asking for a stronger promise is not a promise that has been drawn. Do not add the two into one stock.

A CCIR research note dated the second of October walks through how Nscale's filing describes several ways of borrowing against GPUs. That note is a reading of a filing structure. It is not a missed payment, and it is not a restatement of the August cap.

Spender free cash flow remains the live tension. Amazon's trailing free cash flow is still negative seven point six billion. Alphabet printed negative five point nine billion in the second quarter. Meta printed seven hundred eighty million. Oracle printed negative five point four billion of free cash flow in the just-reported quarter, spent twenty-eight and a half billion of capital, and sold twenty billion of common stock through an at-the-market program. Full-year capital spending is still guided at ninety to ninety-five billion. July's envelopes moved up at three of the four large spenders and none cut them. No later cut has printed.

## What would change the letter

The score moves to four if a named guarantee is restated, if a neocloud misses a payment, if another large cheap-tier cut arrives without a volume offset, or if NVIDIA falls more than fifteen percent from the May high while capital-spending guides stay up.

The score moves to five if a residual-value guarantee is drawn, if a hyperscaler cuts capital spending, or if a hub credit event prints.

The score moves to two only if mid-tier token prices stop falling for two quarters, neocloud rental holds, and the circular book stops growing.

The weekend confirmed a closed tape, official cheap cards that held their lists, and a rental series that advanced one day without leaving the cheap Hopper band. The conversion rate did not recover. Pipeline talk about stronger guarantees is not a draw.

## The open question

The loop is a race between a falling conversion rate and a rising stock of promises. The question worth keeping open is whether tokens sold, at these prices, can service paper that was sized on a higher price.

This letter does not tell the cash account what to own.
