## What would change the letter

The score moves to four if a named guarantee is restated, if a neocloud misses a payment, if another large cheap-tier cut arrives without a volume offset, or if NVIDIA falls more than fifteen percent from the May high while capital-spending guides stay up.

The score moves to five if a residual-value guarantee is drawn, if a hyperscaler cuts capital spending, or if a hub credit event prints.

The score moves to two only if mid-tier token prices stop falling for two quarters, neocloud rental holds, and the circular book stops growing.

Wednesday confirmed a slightly firmer hub mark, a slightly firmer rim equity print, official cheap cards that held their lists, and a rental series that now rests on the twenty-ninth. The conversion rate did not recover.

## The open question

The loop is a race between a falling conversion rate and a rising stock of promises. The question worth keeping open is whether tokens sold, at these prices, can service paper that was sized on a higher price.

This letter does not tell the cash account what to own.
