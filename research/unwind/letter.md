# The Unwind Letter

**12 September 2026**
Danger level: **3, Stressed** — unchanged

This is a research letter, not a trading session and not a recommendation. Nothing in it is a short, a hedge, or an allocation.

---

Good morning.

Friday was a quiet close after Thursday’s Oracle print. NVIDIA finished at two hundred eighteen twenty-nine, almost unchanged on the day and still about seven and three quarters percent below the mid-May high. CoreWeave finished at eighty-eight ninety-nine. The S&P 500 rose on the session and still ended the week lower. Official token cards did not move. Neocloud GPU rental eased a few cents and stayed in the same cheap band.

The conversion-rate race is the same one that set this score on the fourth of September. Cheap-tier list prices remain where they were cut at the end of July. Neocloud hours still clear well below the hyperscaler sticker. No guarantee has been drawn and no rim payment has been missed. The loop is still asking last year’s silicon to service paper that was sized on a higher conversion rate while new paper continues to sit on the books.

The danger level stays at three, Stressed. Friday did not add a new cut, a new guarantee, or a missed payment. It also did not repair free cash flow at the spenders.

## Tokens and the conversion rate

Official provider pages still show the July split. OpenAI lists GPT-5.6 Luna at twenty cents of input and one dollar twenty of output per million tokens. Terra sits at two dollars and twelve dollars. The flagship GPT-6 Astra lists at ten dollars and fifty dollars. Sol remains on promotional pricing of four dollars and twenty dollars through at least the twenty-first of November. Anthropic’s cards put Claude Opus 5 at five and twenty-five, Sonnet 5 at two and ten. Google lists Gemini 3.1 Pro at two and twelve for prompts at or below two hundred thousand tokens, and Gemini 3.8 Flash at an introductory seventy-five cents and three dollars seventy-five through the end of the year. xAI lists Grok 4.6 at two and six below the long-context breakpoint.

These are list prices, not realized revenue. Volume can still rescue the dollar total if buyers consume enough of the cheap tokens. No primary filing has yet shown that volume offset. Until it does, a lower list price remains a lower conversion rate on the installed base that the circular paper was sized against.

## The price of an hour

The CCIR series printed on the eleventh of September put a guaranteed H100 at ten dollars fifty-three an hour on a hyperscaler, three dollars fifty-eight at a neocloud, and three dollars four on the marketplace. H200 printed ten thirty, four thirty-one, and four twenty-one. B200 printed fourteen twenty-four, six sixty-nine, and five eighty. An interruptible H100 at a neocloud still cleared near two fifteen.

Neocloud H100 eased three cents from Thursday’s three sixty-one. That is a move inside the same cheap band, not a new regime. A residual-value guarantee written against a 2025 rental deck is still looking at a different number than the deck that is clearing today. Hyperscaler stickers staying high does not rescue a neocloud that financed at the low print. The first place this race becomes cash is not NVIDIA’s mark. It is a payment date at the rim.

## Circular paper and spender cash

NVIDIA’s August filing still caps residual-value guarantees at the SB Energy Ohio site at one hundred five billion dollars, with OpenAI as the tenant. Amazon has closed fifty billion of OpenAI equity and added one hundred billion of AWS commercial commitment over eight years. That paper did not shrink on Friday. Announced is not funded is not drawn. Drawn is the print that would move this letter from three to five.

Spender free cash flow remains the live tension. Amazon’s trailing free cash flow is still negative seven point six billion. Alphabet printed negative five point nine billion in the second quarter. Meta printed seven hundred eighty million. Oracle printed negative five point four billion of free cash flow in the just-reported quarter, spent twenty-eight and a half billion of capital, and sold twenty billion of common stock through an at-the-market program. Full-year capital spending is still guided at ninety to ninety-five billion. July’s envelopes moved up at three of the four large spenders and none cut them. The loop is still paying for silicon out of the balance sheet and the capital markets rather than out of incremental AI cash.

CoreWeave equity closed at eighty-eight ninety-nine on Friday, a few cents under Thursday and still well below the fifty-two-week high of one hundred fifty-three twenty. Debt stock remains about thirty-five billion. Financing still closed. An equity print is not the same thing as a cheaper refinancing or a lower debt load.

## Hub mark and the collateral

NVIDIA closed at two hundred eighteen twenty-nine on Friday, about seven and three quarters percent below the mid-May high of two hundred thirty-six fifty-four. The semiconductor index finished at eleven thousand eight hundred twenty-four, still far below the June high. The hub mark is the collateral for the next circular round. It remains intact enough that the danger level stays at three rather than moving higher.

## What would change the letter

The score moves to four if a named guarantee is restated, if a neocloud misses a payment, if another large cheap-tier cut arrives without a volume offset, or if NVIDIA falls more than fifteen percent from the May high while capital-spending guides stay up.

The score moves to five if a residual-value guarantee is drawn, if a hyperscaler cuts capital spending, or if a hub credit event prints.

The score moves to two only if mid-tier token prices stop falling for two quarters, neocloud rental holds, and the circular book stops growing.

Thursday confirmed demand and a larger stock of promises. Friday confirmed that the conversion rate did not recover over the weekend that followed.

## The open question

The loop is a race between a falling conversion rate and a rising stock of promises. The question worth keeping open is whether tokens sold, at these prices, can service paper that was sized on a higher price.

This letter does not tell the cash account what to own.
