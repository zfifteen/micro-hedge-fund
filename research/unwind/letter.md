## Circular paper and spender cash

NVIDIA’s August filing still caps residual-value guarantees at the SB Energy Ohio site at one hundred five billion dollars, with OpenAI as the tenant. Amazon has closed fifty billion of OpenAI equity and added one hundred billion of AWS commercial commitment over eight years. That paper did not shrink overnight. Announced is not funded is not drawn. Drawn is the print that would move this letter from three to five.

The first of October Reuters report still sits in a different drawer. Bankers and asset managers, speaking without names, said they want more certainty on a financing plan NVIDIA has described around chip-backed loans, and sources said deals still in the pipeline are likely to carry stronger guarantees. NVIDIA’s own statement said its compute can support long-term financing and that partners assess each opportunity, including residual value. The company has also said some of those deals could carry no more than a twenty-five percent residual-value guarantee. Intent in a pipeline is not the August cap, and a lender asking for a stronger promise is not a promise that has been drawn. Do not add the two into one stock. Commentary from the hub’s chief executive that a named neocloud needs no further backstop is still commentary. It is not a restatement of a filed instrument.

## What would change the letter

The score moves to four if a named guarantee is restated, if a neocloud misses a payment, if another large cheap-tier cut arrives without a volume offset, or if NVIDIA falls more than fifteen percent from the May high while capital-spending guides stay up. A close about ten percent under that high does not, by itself, move the score.

The score moves to five if a residual-value guarantee is drawn, if a hyperscaler cuts capital spending, or if a hub credit event prints.

The score moves to two only if mid-tier token prices stop falling for two quarters, neocloud rental holds, and the circular book stops growing. A marketplace cell still inside a cheap band is not that hold. A Haiku name at ten cents is the other direction.

Thursday’s session and the twenty-first’s rental stamp confirmed a hub close about ten percent under the May high, a semiconductor index about twenty-seven percent under its June high, a rim equity mark about sixty-three percent under its high with unsecured notes at distressed yields, official flagship cards that held their lists, a cheap Haiku name still on the Anthropic card, a new Gemini flagship at an introductory list, and a rental series that left the neocloud H100 cell at three dollars thirty-three. The conversion rate did not recover. Pipeline talk about stronger guarantees is not a draw. Commentary that no further backstop is required is not a filing.

## The open question

The loop is a race between a falling conversion rate and a rising stock of promises. The question worth keeping open is whether tokens sold, at these prices, can service paper that was sized on a higher price.

This letter does not tell the cash account what to own.
