This is a research letter, not a trading session and not a recommendation. Nothing in it is a short, a hedge, or an allocation.

These are list prices, not realized revenue. Volume can still rescue the dollar total if buyers consume enough of the cheap tokens. No primary filing has yet shown that volume offset. Until it does, a lower list price remains a lower conversion rate on the installed base that the circular paper was sized against.

## Hub mark and the collateral

NVIDIA closed at two hundred nineteen thirty-four on Thursday, about seven and three tenths percent below the mid-May high of two hundred thirty-six fifty-four. The semiconductor index finished at eleven thousand five hundred ninety-nine, three percent above Wednesday and still well below the June high of fourteen thousand six hundred fifty-five. The hub mark is the collateral for the next circular round. It is firmer than Wednesday. It is still intact enough that the danger level stays at three rather than moving to four.

## What would change the letter

The score moves to four if a named guarantee is restated, if a neocloud misses a payment, if another large cheap-tier cut arrives without a volume offset, or if NVIDIA falls more than fifteen percent from the May high while capital-spending guides stay up.

The score moves to five if a residual-value guarantee is drawn, if a hyperscaler cuts capital spending, or if a hub credit event prints.

The score moves to two only if mid-tier token prices stop falling for two quarters, neocloud rental holds, and the circular book stops growing.

Thursday confirmed a firmer hub, a weaker rim mark with new issuance rather than a missed coupon, an unchanged list-price stack, and a rental series that is still the sixteenth’s print, now showing three thirty-eight on the public neocloud H100 cell. The conversion rate did not recover.

## The open question

The loop is a race between a falling conversion rate and a rising stock of promises. The question worth keeping open is whether tokens sold, at these prices, can service paper that was sized on a higher price.

This letter does not tell the cash account what to own.
