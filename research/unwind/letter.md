## Circular paper and spender cash

NVIDIA's August filing still caps residual-value guarantees at the SB Energy Ohio site at one hundred five billion dollars, with OpenAI as the tenant. Amazon has closed fifty billion of OpenAI equity and added one hundred billion of AWS commercial commitment over eight years. That paper did not shrink on Monday. Announced is not funded is not drawn. Drawn is the print that would move this letter from three to five.

The first of October Reuters report still sits in a different drawer. Bankers and asset managers, speaking without names, said they want more certainty on a financing plan NVIDIA has described around chip-backed loans, and sources said deals still in the pipeline are likely to carry stronger guarantees. NVIDIA's own statement said its compute can support long-term financing and that partners assess each opportunity, including residual value. The company has also said some of those deals could carry no more than a twenty-five percent residual-value guarantee. Intent in a pipeline is not the August cap, and a lender asking for a stronger promise is not a promise that has been drawn. Do not add the two into one stock.

## What would change the letter

The score moves to four if a named guarantee is restated, if a neocloud misses a payment, if another large cheap-tier cut arrives without a volume offset, or if NVIDIA falls more than fifteen percent from the May high while capital-spending guides stay up. A close above that high does not, by itself, move the score the other way.

The score moves to five if a residual-value guarantee is drawn, if a hyperscaler cuts capital spending, or if a hub credit event prints.

The score moves to two only if mid-tier token prices stop falling for two quarters, neocloud rental holds, and the circular book stops growing. A sixteen-cent slip in the neocloud H100 cell is a move against that hold.

Monday confirmed a hub close above the May high, official cheap cards that held their lists, a research billing start that is now a day old and still not a flagship cut, and a rental series that advanced one day and left the recent cheap Hopper band on the downside. The conversion rate did not recover. Pipeline talk about stronger guarantees is not a draw.

## The open question

The loop is a race between a falling conversion rate and a rising stock of promises. The question worth keeping open is whether tokens sold, at these prices, can service paper that was sized on a higher price.

This letter does not tell the cash account what to own.
