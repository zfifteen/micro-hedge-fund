Announced is not funded is not drawn. Drawn is the print that would move this letter from three to five.

The first of October Reuters report still sits in a different drawer. ## What would change the letter

The score moves to four if a named guarantee is restated, if a neocloud misses a payment, if another large cheap-tier cut arrives without a volume offset, or if NVIDIA falls more than fifteen percent from the May high while capital-spending guides stay up. A close back under that high does not, by itself, move the score.

The score moves to five if a residual-value guarantee is drawn, if a hyperscaler cuts capital spending, or if a hub credit event prints.

The score moves to two only if mid-tier token prices stop falling for two quarters, neocloud rental holds, and the circular book stops growing. A marketplace cell twelve cents firmer, inside a still-cheap band, is not that hold. A new Haiku name at ten cents is the other direction.

Thursday confirmed a hub close back under the May high, a semiconductor index about fourteen percent under its June high, a rim equity mark about forty-seven percent under its high, official flagship cards that held their lists, a new cheap Haiku name on the Anthropic card, and a rental series that advanced two days and left the neocloud H100 cell unchanged at three dollars thirty-four. The conversion rate did not recover. Pipeline talk about stronger guarantees is not a draw.

## The open question

The loop is a race between a falling conversion rate and a rising stock of promises. The question worth keeping open is whether tokens sold, at these prices, can service paper that was sized on a higher price.

This letter does not tell the cash account what to own.
