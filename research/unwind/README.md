# research/unwind

Opened 2026-09-03. This folder is a process file, not a trading session. Presence of a note here is not a short, not a hedge, and not an allocation.

This folder is **open-ended**. A hypothesis can fail without closing the work. H4 failing as regime change does not end the watch. The object under study is the loop, not a date.

## Topic

The working hypothesis under test is that AI-linked equity prices embed a capex-and-financing loop that cannot be sustained at the current second derivative. The first binding constraint, if it exists, shows up in cash flow and credit, not in whether the technology is real.

The loop is concurrent, not sequential. Token prices, GPU rental, residual-value guarantees, take-or-pay offtake, hyperscaler free cash flow, rim credit, and hub equity marks update on different clocks. That is the race condition. A later print can invalidate an earlier one before the earlier one has amortized.

Split claims. Do not treat "the stock market," "AI bubble," "token prices are falling," and "unwind has begun" as one object.

Standing claims:

- H1 — index concentration / deconcentration
- H2 — hyperscaler capex vs incremental cash flow
- H3 — financing architecture (vendor finance, take-or-pay, private credit, circular deals)
- H4 — July–August 2026 was a regime change, not an air pocket inside an intact boom
- H5 — listed token prices and GPU rental are the cash conversion of installed silicon; deflation there can jeopardize paper that was sized on a higher conversion rate

H4 remains the weakest regime claim and is tested first. H5 is the open-ended claim. Keep measuring whether the output price of the loop is falling faster than the tenor of the paper that funded the last generation.

## The loop (one paragraph)

Hub equity marks and vendor equity stakes fund, or collateralize, GPU and site capex. Capex ships silicon into hyperscalers and neoclouds. Those operators sell tokens and GPU-hours. Token list prices and rental rates are the only cash the installed base actually prints. Residual-value guarantees and take-or-pay contracts assume a conversion rate. If token and rental prices gap down while the next generation's guarantees are already booked, the prior generation has not paid for itself and the new paper is already live. That is the race.

## Rules for this folder

- Use primary sources first. Filings, guidance, credit prints, provider rate cards, and observable rental series come before commentary. Commentary is hypothesis.
- Label every input Measured or Hypothesis.
- Write the daily report and analysis in easy-to-understand, grammatically correct, complete sentences. Tables may hold numbers. Judgments, danger-level reasons, and snapshot logs must be full sentences.
- Research is not a book. A confirmed path still has to be expressible on the agentic cash account before it becomes a trade.
- Do not write PII. Use last-4 only. Do not write emails, names, order UUIDs, or connector dumps.
- Cash remains a valid position while this work is open.
- Do not close the folder because a single hypothesis failed. Add a new question instead of declaring the subject finished.
