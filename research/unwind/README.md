# research/unwind

Opened 2026-09-03. Process folder, not a trading session. Presence of a note here is not a short, not a hedge, and not an allocation.

## Topic

Working hypothesis under test: AI-linked equity prices embed a capex-and-financing loop that cannot be sustained at the current second derivative. The first binding constraint, if it exists, shows up in cash flow and credit, not in whether the technology is real.

Split claims. Do not treat "the stock market," "AI bubble," and "unwind has begun" as one object.

- H1 — index concentration / deconcentration
- H2 — hyperscaler capex vs incremental cash flow
- H3 — financing architecture (vendor finance, take-or-pay, private credit, circular deals)
- H4 — July–August 2026 was a regime change, not an air pocket inside an intact boom

H4 is the weakest claim and is tested first.

## Rules for this folder

- Primary sources first. Filings, guidance, credit prints, utilization, order books. Commentary is hypothesis.
- Label every input Measured or Hypothesis.
- Research is not a book. A confirmed path still has to be expressible on the agentic cash account before it becomes a trade.
- No PII. Last-4 only. No emails, names, order UUIDs, or connector dumps.
- Cash remains a valid position while this work is open.
