**H2 judgment:** Negative FCF on huge capex is an H2 cash-stress **CHECK**, not a floor-4 event. It does guide FY27 **total revenue at least $90B** and non-GAAP EPS **$8.10**, and it confirms capital-raise plans continue. Under the prep move table, that is the **hold danger 3** path — not the cut path.

## 5. Official danger recommendation

**Keep danger at 3 (Stressed).**

Reasons, in full sentences:

1. The prep memo’s hold-at-3 path printed: demand language intact, FCF still compressed, and no FY27 capex **cut** disclosed in the primary release.
2. No Measured floor-4/5 event from `clocks.yaml` / `loop-health.md` fired: no named guarantee drawn, no take-or-pay miss, no hub or rim payment miss, no hyperscaler capex cut.
3. Negative FCF of about $5.4B on about $28.5B of capex is an H2 cash-stress check. Per the honesty wall and the prep bars, that check keeps H2 live; it does not by itself raise official danger to 4.
4. Narrative, stock bounce, and secondary “they will default” chatter do not move danger.

This recommendation matches the standing `loop-health.md` daily report after the print (danger held at 3). This scorecard does not edit `loop-health.md`, `hypotheses.md`, `state/`, or trading rules.
