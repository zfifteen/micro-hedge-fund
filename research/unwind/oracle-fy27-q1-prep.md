## Why this print is on the Unwind calendar

Oracle is the named next forced cash print in `hypotheses.md` and `capex-cash.md`. It sits on three clocks at once:

1. **Spender / builder cash (H2).** FY26 FCF was **−$23.686B** on OCF **$31.977B** and capex **$55.663B** (capex/OCF **1.74**). FY27 capex guide is **$90–95B**. RPO is **$638B**. Prepaid / customer-supplied hardware on large AI contracts is **$75B** (IR, as cited in `capex-cash.md`).
2. **Rim credit (H3-adjacent).** Oracle is investment-grade but thin (**BBB−** in secondary commentary). A rim print can move before a hub miss.
3. **OpenAI concentration (Hypothesis chain).** Secondary commentary (Steve Eisman, week ending 2026-09-04) claims roughly half of Oracle RPO is OpenAI-linked and that OpenAI unit economics are still cash-negative. That is **not** a packing Measured row until Oracle’s own disclosure or a filing-backed IR exhibit says so. Treat it as the question the print may answer, not as a fact already on the board.

Oracle cannot, by itself, unwrite the July cheap-tier token cut or the August **$105B** NVDA residual-value cap. Those clocks stay live either way (`letter.md`, `loop-health.md`).
