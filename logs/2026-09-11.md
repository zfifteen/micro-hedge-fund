## Lessons for the Larger Fund
- Explicit, time-boxed cash constraints (e.g., “hold until after CPI”) create clean decision points. Once the constraint expires, the residual should be re-evaluated rather than allowed to drift into permanent cash.
- Half-size deployment of a residual sleeve preserves optionality for the next clustered event (FOMC) while still putting capital to work. Full deployment or full hold both discard information that the constraint itself encoded.
- What would scale: the same post-event reallocation logic. What would break at larger size is treating a single CPI print as a mandate to invent activity in the absence of primary-source edge.
